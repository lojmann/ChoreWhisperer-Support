# ChoreWhisperer Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-11-08**

ChoreWhisperer does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no account and no sign-in. Chore lists, recurrence rules, and completion history are stored locally on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account via CloudKit. Household member display names are provided by Apple and shown in-app only. Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
