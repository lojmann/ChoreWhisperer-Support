# ChoreWhisperer — Support

ChoreWhisperer is an iPhone and iPad app for shared household recurring chores. Pick recurring chores from a catalog or add your own, define how often each repeats — on a fixed weekday schedule or an interval counted from the last completion — and get a push notification when it's due, with snooze. Reminders live in lists a household can share: everyone invited can see, complete, snooze, edit, and add. There is no in-app login — your device's iCloud account powers sync and sharing, and the app works fully offline without one.

## Get help

The public support contact for ChoreWhisperer is this repository's **Discussions** page: <https://github.com/lojmann/ChoreWhisperer-Support/discussions>. Open a discussion there to ask a question or report a problem.

## Privacy

Read the [Privacy Policy](privacy). ChoreWhisperer collects no personal data: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
